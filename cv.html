PROFILE

Born on the French west coast, I grew up close to the ocean. During my bachelor's degree I visited reef island ecosystems and saw how vulnerable they are to climate change, which led me to a Ph.D. on coastal protection in French Polynesia, focused on benthic communities and how they function. Today I quantify shifts in ecosystem functioning under global change, combining fieldwork, experiments and advanced statistical modelling across spatial scales. I also love data analysis and visualisation, and using them to communicate complex findings clearly.
