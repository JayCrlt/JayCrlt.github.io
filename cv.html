PROFILE

Born on the French west coast, my childhood connection to the ocean fuelled my dedication to marine environments. During my bachelor's degree, I had the opportunity to visit reef island ecosystems, where I sadly witnessed their vulnerability to climate change. I then pursued a Ph.D. on coastal protection in French Polynesia, focusing on benthic functionality and communities. My main interest lies in quantifying shifts in ecosystem functioning due to global change, through a combination of fieldwork, experiments and advanced statistical modelling across multiple spatial scales. I also enjoy data analysis and visualisation, aiming to communicate complex findings in clear and accessible ways.

RESEARCH PROJECTS

I lead two projects as principal investigator. MEDSHIFTS (since 2026, with J. Garrabou) studies Mediterranean shifts in macrophyte functioning and is funded by my Beatriu de Pinós and Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships (~371,000 €). MEDSYNERGIES (2024, with N. Teixidó) explores the synergies of benthic communities to ocean acidification (~3,675 €).

I have also contributed to four larger collaborative projects: B-USEFUL (2024, PI M. Lindegren, ~4.6 M€), on user-oriented solutions to monitor and manage biodiversity and ecosystem services in vulnerable European seas; 4OCEANS (2021, PI N. Teixidó, ~745,000 €), on predicting future oceans under climate change; SCORE-REEF (2020, PIs M. Adjeroud & V. Parravicini, ~220,000 €), on the spatio-temporal variability of coral reefs at the global scale; and REEF SERVICES (2018, PI V. Parravicini, ~800,000 €), on the consequences of global warming for coral reefs and the services they provide.

TEACHING

I teach at three levels. At the Stazione Zoologica di Napoli (SZN), I lead a summer-school course in temperate marine benthic ecology, Benthic physiology & processes: from field to data analysis (2025).

For the CNRS, CESAB, CSIC and SZN, I run hands-on sessions on reproducible science & statistics: GitHub and Markdown as core tools for a research workflow (summer school, 2025), reproducible science with GitHub (2023, 2025), fully reproducible meta-analyses in R (2023), Bayesian statistics in ecology (2024) and connecting Zotero with Obsidian (2025).

Since 2016, I have also been a university lecturer in the coral reef MSc programme of the École Pratique des Hautes Études (EPHE), teaching functional ecology, coral growth and coastal protection, and diversity–habitat relationships on reefs, and recording two MOOCs, on coastal protection and reef functioning, and on fish distribution in French Polynesia.

SUPERVISION

I am an advisor on the Ph.D. of Gaia Grasso (since 2024, main supervisors N. Teixidó, S. Comeau & U. Cardini), on marine biodiversity and ecosystem function of benthic habitats in a changing ocean using novel underwater imaging approaches.

I have also supervised five MSc students: Karla Abril Hernández Ramírez (2024), on ocean acidification effects on coastal marine ecosystems; Romane Torchy (2023), on a meta-analysis of benthic functioning in the face of global change; Cyril Hautecoeur (2021), on the lack of vertical reef accretion to keep up with future sea-level rise; and Martin Alessandrini & Hmeniko Tourancheau (2019), on the growth of three coral species (A. hyacinthus, P. verrucosa and P. lutea) in a context of climate change.

TALKS

I have presented my work at the International Coral Reef Symposium (Bremen, 2021 & 2022), the European Coral Reef Symposium (Naples, 2024), where I also co-chaired the session on shallow temperate reefs, and the World Conference on Marine Biodiversity (Bruges, 2026). These talks covered carbonate production by juvenile corals during reef recovery (2021), how the loss of coral structural complexity threatens coastlines (2022), species trait impairment after Mediterranean mortality events (2024) and how two decades of fish community change reshape biogeochemical functioning (2026). In Bruges, I also presented A. Gran et al.'s work on biodiversity change–pressure relationships across European seas.

As a co-author, I contributed to talks given by colleagues at the International Symposium on the Ocean in a High-CO2 World (Wellington, 2026), the World Conference on Marine Biodiversity (Bruges, 2026), the European Coral Reef Symposium (Naples, 2024), the European Conference on Scientific Diving (Heraklion, 2024), the International Temperate Reefs Symposium (Hobart, 2023) and the Western Society of Naturalists (Monterey, 2023), on natural CO2 vents, crustose coralline algae, macrophyte collapses, coralligenous communities and the T-MEDNet network.

I have been invited to speak at the Instituto Español de Oceanografía (IEO) in Palma (2020 & 2025), the Marine Ecology Data Analysis and Synthesis (MEDAS) center in Ischia and the Island Research Center and Environmental Observatory (CRIOBE) in Perpignan (2022), and the Laboratoire d'Océanographie de Villefranche-sur-Mer (LOV) (2024), on topics ranging from coastal protection scenarios for reef islands to functional shifts in Mediterranean fish communities.
