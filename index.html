ABOUT ME

JÉRÉMY T. CARLOT
MARINE BENTHIC ECOLOGIST

I am a post-doctoral fellow with the Institut de Ciènces del Mar at CSIC in Spain. I am mostly interested in measuring the physiological traits of marine organisms (e.g., calcification or net photosynthesis for benthic organisms, nutrients uptake for fish) to quantify how the ecosystem functioning is changing in the face of global change. My works involve a combination of fieldwork, in situ and ex-situ experiments, and advanced statistical modeling across local and large spatial scales.
