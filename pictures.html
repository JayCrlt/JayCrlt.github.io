MY PICTURES
I've been lucky enough to spend most of my life close to the sea, and to travel for work as much as for pleasure. Here are some of the places I've passed through along the way, mostly around water and islands: from the lagoons of French Polynesia to the Balearic and Canary Islands, the volcanic coasts of Ischia and the Aeolian Islands, the Atlantic shores of France, and the cliffs and fjords of Iceland and Norway. Some were taken during fieldwork, most simply because the light was right.
